🌌 GALAXY WARFARE 🌌

A Real-Time Space Strategy & Conquest Game

Pick one of 12 rival nations, build up your homeworld, and fight for control of the galaxy - against the other 11 nations, hostile Cyborg outposts, and a swarming alien Zoonester hive.

▶ Play Now How To Play

⚔️ Real-Time Fleet Combat

Command aircraft, naval warships, and ground forces at once. Drag-select your fleet, click an enemy to send them in and attack-move there automatically, or click open space to reposition. Every unit has its own HP, attack power, range, and speed.

🌍 12 Playable Nations

USA, China, Russia, UK, France, Japan, Germany, India, Brazil, Australia, Canada, and Italy each start with their own homeworld and a unique set of combat bonuses - see the Nations tab for the full breakdown.

👽 Alien & Cyborg Threats

The galaxy isn't just 12 nations fighting each other. Cyborg strongholds, a Zoonester alien hive, and rival-controlled outposts add hostile territory you'll have to fight through or around.

🎖️ Campaign Mode

Pick one nation and fight through 12 escalating stages against every other rival in sequence, with alien and Cyborg objectives mixed in along the way and a final stage that's aliens only. Progress autosaves so you can pick up where you left off.

🗺️ Minimap & Full Map Control

A live minimap shows your units and the surrounding galaxy at a glance - drag-select or click on it to command your fleet without hunting around the main view.

💾 Autosave

Standard games and campaign runs both autosave as you play, so a closed tab or a multi-hour session never costs you progress.

HOW TO PLAY

Everything you need to command your fleet and take the galaxy

Getting Started

From the start screen, choose Standard Game to play free-for-all against every other planet in the galaxy, or Campaign Mode to pick one nation and fight the other 11 in a scripted, escalating story. Either way, you'll pick a difficulty (Easy, Normal, or Hard, which scales how often and how aggressively the AI builds, moves, and attacks) and a nation to play as. You start with a homeworld, a starting garrison, and a trickle of resources - everything else is built from there.

Controls

Selecting units

  • Click-and-drag a box over your units (a normal left-click drag) to select all of them.
  • Click a single unit's entry in the sidebar unit list to select just that one.
  • You can also drag-select directly on the minimap.

Moving and attacking

  • With units selected, click empty space to send them there.
  • With units selected, click an enemy ship, ground unit, or building to attack it. If it's already in range they fire immediately; if it's farther away, your units will automatically travel there first (an attack-move order) and open fire the moment they're in range - no need to move manually and then attack again.
  • An attack-move order keeps re-engaging the same target every turn until it's destroyed, unless you give the unit a new move or attack order.
  • The MOVE and ATTACK buttons in the sidebar arm the same behavior explicitly if you'd rather choose the mode yourself before clicking.
  • Right-click and drag to pan the camera. A right-click with no drag deselects your current selection.

Minimap

  • Shows your units (green dots, yellow when selected) and the surrounding galaxy.
  • Drag-select on the minimap to select units, then click elsewhere on it to send them there - useful for issuing orders across the map without scrolling.

Camera

  • 🏠 HOME recenters the camera on your homeworld.
  • 🗺️ VIEW ALL zooms out to see the whole battle.

Economy & Buildings

Every playable homeworld has 6 buildings: two nation-flavored structures, a Harbor, a Marine Base, a War Factory, and a Defense Gun. Buildings have 5 HP each (20 HP for the Defense Gun, which also auto-fires at any enemy unit that wanders within 500 range - 15 damage per shot). Resources accumulate automatically over time and are spent building new units from the sidebar's build menu. Losing all of your buildings ends the game, so keep some defenders home even while you're on the offensive.

Ground Invasion: the Harbor System

Ground units (Groundpounders, Ironbeast, Boomcannon) can't fly or sail - they're bound to whatever planet they're standing on. To invade another planet, build a Cargohauler (it carries up to 10 ground troops), sail it near your harbor or your deployed troops to load them, sail it to an enemy planet, and unload them onto the beach to begin the ground assault.

Combat Rules

  • Every unit can attack once per turn - after firing, it's locked in place until the next turn resets it.
  • Range, HP, and attack power vary a lot by unit type - check the Units tab before you commit a fleet.
  • Some fighters (Thunderwing, Shadowglider) can't target certain fast interceptors (Blazefalcon, Skyripper, Vortexhawk, Phantom Strike, Razorwing) - pair your fleet with something that can.
  • Ground units can only fight other ground units.
  • Frostwing attacks freeze the target in place for 2 turns instead of dealing heavy damage.
  • Phantom Strike can fire a slow, shootable long-range missile (25 damage, 2000 range) once its cooldown is up, or fall back to its regular gun (50 damage) - you'll be asked to choose when both are available.

Campaign Mode

Pick one of the 12 nations and fight through 12 stages, one per rival nation, in sequence - later stages add more planets (some stages have as many as 7 objective planets), enemy-garrisoned outposts, and Cyborg alien bases you have to clear along the way. Each stage lists its objectives up front (visible in the collapsible Objectives panel during play) - typically something like "take out their outpost, destroy the alien base on the way in, then take their homeworld." The final stage is an all-aliens gauntlet. Progress autosaves after every stage clear, so you can close the game and resume later from the start screen. Losing a stage offers a Retry Stage instead of restarting the whole campaign.

Difficulty & Saving

Easy / Normal / Hard scale how often the AI builds, moves, and attacks, and how aggressive the special enemy factions (Cyborg, Zoonester, Roufestreal) are on top of their own baseline. Both standard games and campaign runs autosave as you play; a manual Save/Load to file is also available from the in-game menu if you want to keep more than one game going.

Hostile-Only Units

These don't appear in your build menu - you'll only ever face them as enemies.

Cyborg Dreadnought

300 HP, 210 attack, 500 range. The heavy backbone of Cyborg strongholds - approach in force.

Zoon Parasite

200 HP, 500 range, doesn't fire a normal gun but is extremely fast (speed 5.0) and swarms aggressively.

Roufestreal

100 HP, lays space mines that detonate on anything that strays too close instead of attacking directly.

Beyond the 12 Nations

The galaxy also holds hostile territory that isn't playable but that you'll have to fight:

🦾 Cyborg Strongholds

Four fortified Cyborg planets that build and defend with Cyborg Dreadnoughts.

👽 Zoonester Hive

An alien homeworld that swarms with fast, aggressive Zoon Parasites.

💣 Roufestreal Command

A minelayer stronghold that seeds space with hidden mines instead of fighting head-on.

🏴 Rival Outposts

Garrisoned satellite planets belonging to a rival nation - clear them out on the way to that nation's homeworld.
